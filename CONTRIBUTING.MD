# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this project.

At the current stage of this project, this repository is published for **reference and transparency only**.

**External contributions are currently not accepted** - pull requests, feature requests, or unsolicited code changes.


## Pull Requests

Please **do not open pull requests**. Any pull requests submitted may be closed without review.

## Issues

Unless otherwise stated, issue reports and feature requests are also not being accepted.

To contribute, please provide your feedback directly on [official Space Haven Discord's modding channel](https://discord.com/invite/spacehaven).

## Forking

You are welcome to fork this repository for your own personal or educational use, subject to the project's license.

Please do not expect upstream changes or support.

## Future Contributions

The contribution policy may change in the future.

If external contributions become welcome, this document will be updated accordingly.

Thank you for your understanding and your interest in the project.
